## 💡 Exam-Taking Tips

1. **Read questions carefully** - Look for nuanced keywords (cost, performance, compliance, scalability)
2. **Governance is complex** - Management Groups, Policies, custom RBAC roles are frequently asked
3. **Architecture is contextual** - No one "right answer"; consider scenario requirements
4. **Data flows matter** - Understand data movement, transformation, and destination options
5. **Trade-offs are key** - AZ-305 tests your ability to balance cost, security, performance, and operations
